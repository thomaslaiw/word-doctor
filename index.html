Activity briefing

You are the word doctors.

Six sentences. Each one has a sick word. Work as a class to find the problem.
Correct answer: +10. Confident wrong answer: −5.

After the class decides, you will also see what the AI thinks. It has read the same sentence. It does not know your answer.

For the teacher — not for projection. Learners need no devices: the class answers aloud or on mini-whiteboards, and you record the decision on screen. The AI panel stays hidden until you choose to show it. Full session = the lesson. Short demo = 5 minutes, for the presentation.
